# Sayang Intan Berlian

## Company Profile & Service Guide

**Sayang Intan Berlian (SIB)** is a heritage luxury Thai–Malay jewelry manufacturer. The company is a fourth-generation family business with more than 50 years of jewelry craftsmanship heritage.

SIB combines inherited Thai–Malay artistic traditions with contemporary craftsmanship to create jewelry with artistic value, personal stories, and the potential to become a family heirloom.

## Brand positioning

SIB specializes in handmade, custom-made, and unique jewelry designs that reflect each customer’s identity. The brand believes jewelry should be more than decoration: it should preserve value and stories across generations.

## Products and services

### Ready stock

Jewelry available for immediate purchase.

### Custom made

New jewelry designed and produced according to customer requirements and budget.

**Supported metals:** Gold 9K, Gold 10K, Gold 14K, Gold 18K, and Platinum.

**Stone options:** Natural Diamond, Lab-Grown Diamond, Moissanite, CZ, and customer-owned diamonds or gemstones for remaking or resetting.

**Indicative price range:** RM100–RM10,000++, depending on budget and design details.

## Design identity

The principal inspiration comes from Thai and Malay art passed down within the family since the great-grandparents’ generation.

Design directions include Antique, Vintage, Handmade, Heritage, and Family Heirloom. SIB can create jewelry based on a customer’s family story.

## Service process

1. Customer shares requirements.
2. Consultation takes place online or at the customer’s location.
3. The design is developed to fit the budget.
4. A sketch or 3D design is prepared.
5. Revisions are unlimited before production begins.
6. A minimum deposit of 50% is paid.
7. Production takes approximately two weeks to two months, depending on complexity.
8. The piece passes quality control.
9. It is delivered with product warranty.
